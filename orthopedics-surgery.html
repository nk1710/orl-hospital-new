Orthopaedic Surgery is a medical specialty that focuses on the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, tendons, and spine. At ORL International Hospital, our Orthopaedics Department provides advanced care to help patients recover from pain, injuries, and mobility problems. Whether it’s a simple fracture or a complex joint issue, our expert orthopaedic surgeons ensure accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

What Are the Types of Orthopaedic Surgery?

1. Joint Replacement Surgery

Used for severe joint damage or arthritis.
Common surgeries include:

  • Knee Replacement

  • Hip Replacement

  • Shoulder Replacement

2. Arthroscopy (Keyhole Surgery)

A minimally invasive procedure to diagnose and treat joint problems, especially in:

  • Knee

  • Shoulder

  • Elbow

  • Ankle

3. Fracture & Trauma Surgery

Used to repair broken bones using plates, screws, rods, or casts.

4. Spine Surgery

For severe spine disorders such as:

  • Herniated disc

  • Nerve compression

  • Chronic back pain

  • Spinal deformities

Common procedures include spinal decompression and spinal fusion.

5. Sports Injury Surgery

Treats injuries like:

  • ACL or ligament tears

  • Meniscus injuries

  • Rotator cuff tears

6. Pediatric Orthopaedic Surgery

For children with congenital bone disorders, limb deformities, or growth issues.

What Conditions Does Orthopaedic Surgery Treat or Manage?

An orthopedic surgeon specializes in diagnosing and treating conditions related to your bones, joints, and muscles. They commonly treat:

  • Bone fractures

  • Joint diseases and disorders

  • Ligament and tendon tears

  • Back pain and spinal problems

  • Knee, hip, and shoulder issues

  • Sports injuries

  • Childhood bone and growth deformities

  • Arthritis-related joint damage

  • Trauma and accident-related injuries

Risks and Benefits of Orthopaedic Surgery

Risks

While orthopaedic surgery is generally safe, potential risks include:

  • Infection

  • Bleeding

  • Blood clots

  • Nerve or vessel injury

  • Reaction to anesthesia

  • Delayed bone healing

These risks are minimized with advanced techniques and expert surgeons at ORL International Hospital.

What Are the Advantages of Orthopaedic Surgery?

Orthopaedic surgery offers several benefits, including:

  • Significant pain relief

  • Improved mobility

  • Correction of bone or joint deformities

  • Better joint function

  • Faster recovery from injuries

  • Long-term relief for chronic conditions

  • Increased independence in daily life

  • Enhanced athletic performance

What Are Musculoskeletal Issues?

Musculoskeletal issues refer to problems affecting the muscles, bones, joints, ligaments, tendons, and spine.
Common examples include:

  • Back pain

  • Neck pain

  • Muscle strain

  • Joint stiffness

  • Arthritis

  • Bone fractures

  • Sciatica

  • Sports injuries

These issues can affect daily movements, cause pain, and reduce quality of life.

What Does an Orthopedic Surgeon Do?

An orthopedic surgeon:

  • Evaluates and identifies problems related to bones, joints, muscles, and ligaments

  • Suggests non-surgical options such as medicines, physical therapy, and lifestyle modifications

  • Conducts surgical procedures including joint replacements, fracture corrections, arthroscopy, and spine-related operations

  • Offers post-treatment and rehabilitation support

  • Works to improve mobility, restore function, and alleviate pain

At ORL International Hospital, our orthopaedic team uses modern diagnostic tools, minimally invasive techniques, and evidence-based protocols to ensure faster recovery and long-lasting relief.
