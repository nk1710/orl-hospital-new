When Should You Visit an Experienced ENT Doctor?

Seek immediate consultation with an ENT specialist if you face any of the following issues:

✔ Difficulty breathing
✔ Hearing loss or ringing in the ears
✔ Sinus pain & headache
✔ Voice changes
✔ Repeated ear or throat infections
