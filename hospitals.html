Company Profile

ORL International Hospital

ORL International Hospital, Jasola is a trusted Multi Specialty Hospital delivering quality healthcare for over 25 years. Located at the heart of Jasola—an important gateway connecting Delhi NCR, Noida, and Faridabad—our hospital plays a vital role in serving the growing healthcare needs of the region. Its strategic position along the highway makes ORL a lifeline for emergency and trauma cases, where timely treatment during the golden hour can save lives.

For decades, ORL International Hospital has been known for its excellence in ENT & Neck Surgery, backed by advanced medical technology and a highly experienced team of specialists. Our commitment to world-class care extends across a wide range of departments including OPD/IPD Services, Orthopaedics & Spine Care, Dental, Gastroenterology, Ophthalmology, Oncology, Internal Medicine, Cardiology (CN & ECG), Urology, Nephrology, Dermatology, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, Laboratory Services, Audiometry, and Physiotherapy.

We take pride in offering patient-centric care with a focus on safety, comfort, and medical transparency. With modern diagnostic facilities and minimally invasive treatment options, patients receive accurate assessments and effective care plans tailored to their needs.

To support patients from all backgrounds, the hospital provides a variety of room options including economy, standard, twin-sharing, and single deluxe rooms—allowing families to choose care that fits their comfort and affordability.

Our proximity to international guest houses and our reputation for specialized treatment make ORL International Hospital a rising destination for medical tourism. Every patient who walks through our doors is treated with compassion, expertise, and the assurance of trusted medical excellence.

At ORL International Hospital, Jasola, our mission is simple—to deliver reliable, accessible, and advanced healthcare that transforms lives.
