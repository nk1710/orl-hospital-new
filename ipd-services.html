IPD Services

IPD means In-Patient Care Treatment Department. It is the hospital facility where the patients are admitted to be observed, diagnosed, treated, operated, or under-taken to provide them with long-term care by the supervision of health professionals. The IPD Services are medical services in hospitals which are offered to patients requiring round-the-clock medical attention, special procedures, and inpatient services. These services will consist of the admission, room set-up, nursing services, medication services, post-operative services and daily doctor visits to the diagnostic service. Our IPD services at ORL international hospital guarantees efficient and patient-centered care that is safe and is backed by superior infrastructure and well trained medical staffs.

When Are IPD Services Needed?

Patients are admitted to IPD when they require:

  • Continuous medical supervision

  • Major or minor surgeries

  • Critical care and emergency monitoring

  • Severe infections or chronic condition management

  • Advanced diagnostic evaluations

  • Post-operative recovery and rehabilitation

  • Accident or trauma care

If a patient’s condition cannot be managed through OPD visits alone, IPD services become essential.

What Services Are Provided Under IPD?

In-Patient Care Treatment  at ORL International Hospital includes a complete range of inpatient facilities, such as:

✔ 24/7 Nursing Care - Skilled nurses monitor vitals, give medications, and support daily activities.

✔ Specialist Doctor Rounds - Daily visits by consultants and on-call doctors for continuous evaluation.

✔ State-of-the-Art Rooms & Wards - Private rooms, semi-private rooms, general wards, and ICU facilities.

✔ Advanced Diagnostics - On-site X-ray, Ultrasound, ECG, Laboratory Tests, and more.

✔ Medication & Pain Management - Accurate dosage monitoring and treatment-based medication plans.

✔ Surgical Care - Pre-operative and post-operative care with full monitoring.

✔ ICU & Critical Care - For patients requiring intensive monitoring and life-saving treatments.

✔ Emergency & Trauma Support - Immediate admission for severe conditions.

✔ Dietary & Nutritional Support - Customized meal planning based on patient's health needs.

✔ Rehabilitation & Physiotherapy - Post-surgery recovery and long-term care assistance.

How Does IPD Work in a Hospital?

The IPD workflow at ORL International Hospital is designed to be smooth, systematic, and patient-friendly:

  1. Admission Process - Patients are admitted after doctor recommendation or emergency evaluation.

  2. Room Allotment - Based on patient condition, preference, and availability.

  3. Assessment & Treatment Plan - Doctors create a personalized treatment protocol.

  4. Daily Monitoring - Nurses track vitals and doctors review progress regularly.

  5. Diagnostic Support - Tests and scans conducted as needed.

  6. Treatment Execution - Medication, surgery, therapy, and recovery management.

  7. Discharge Process - Final evaluation, medical advice, and prescription are provided.

  8. Post-Discharge Follow-up - OPD visits planned to ensure long-term recovery.

Importance of IPD in the Healthcare System

IPD plays a vital role by offering:

  • Advanced medical care for serious conditions

  • Continuous monitoring for high-risk patients

  • Safe surgical & post-surgical management

  • Immediate access to diagnostics and emergency care

  • Efficient treatment of chronic and acute diseases

  • Better recovery outcomes due to supervised care

IPD ensures that patients receive comprehensive, controlled, and timely treatment that cannot be provided through OPD consultations alone.

Key Differences Between OPD and IPD Treatment

Factors OPD (Out-Patient Department) IPD (In-Patient Department)
Definition Patients visit for check-ups without staying in hospital Patients are admitted for observation, treatment, or surgery
Hospital Stay No stay required Stay required (hours/days)
Treatment Type Basic check-ups, diagnostics, prescriptions Advanced treatment, surgeries, continuous care
Monitoring Limited 24/7 monitoring
Cost Generally low Higher due to hospitalization and facilities
Suitable For Mild symptoms, routine follow-ups Severe conditions, emergencies, post-operative care
Facilities Used OPD rooms, labs Rooms/wards, ICU, diagnostics, nursing care
