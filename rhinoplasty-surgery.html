Rhinoplasty Surgery

Rhinoplasty Surgery, also known as nose reshaping surgery, is a procedure that improves the shape, size, and overall structure of the nose. It is done to enhance facial balance, correct breathing issues, or rebuild the nose after injury. The surgery offers both cosmetic nose surgery benefits and functional improvements like nasal breathing enhancement.

Performed By

At ORL International Hospital, Rhinoplasty is performed by highly experienced and trained ENT specialists and the best nose surgeons who focus on safe results and natural-looking nose surgery outcomes.

Why It’s Done

Rhinoplasty is recommended for:

  • Improving the aesthetic appearance of the nose

  • Correcting nasal deformities or birth defects

  • Enhancing breathing by fixing internal nasal issues

  • Repairing injuries through nose reconstruction

  • Adjusting concerns like wide nostrils, crooked nose, or nose bridge correction

Types of Rhinoplasty

  • Open Rhinoplasty – For major reshaping of the nasal structure

  • Closed Rhinoplasty – For subtle corrections with no external scar

  • Functional Rhinoplasty – To improve breathing

  • Aesthetic Rhinoplasty – For cosmetic improvements

  • Revision Rhinoplasty – To correct previous nose surgeries

  • Tip Refinement Surgery / Nose Tip Refinement – Enhancing nasal tip shape

  • Nasal Hump Removal Surgery – Removing bumps on the bridge

Indications for Rhinoplasty Surgery

You may need Rhinoplasty if you have:

  • A crooked or curved nose

  • Wide or bulbous nasal tip

  • Nasal hump or bump

  • Breathing difficulties

  • Deviated septum (may combine with septoplasty)

  • Trauma or injury-related deformities

  • Desire for aesthetic nose enhancement

Contraindications for Rhinoplasty

Rhinoplasty may not be suitable for:

  • Individuals with uncontrolled medical conditions

  • Patients under 16 years (may vary based on growth)

  • Pregnant women

  • People with unrealistic expectations

  • Active nasal infections

Do’s and Don’ts Before & After Rhinoplasty Surgery

Do’s Don’ts
Follow your doctor’s pre-surgery instructions Don’t smoke or consume alcohol before/after surgery
Keep your face clean and avoid makeup on surgery day Don’t touch or press your nose
Take prescribed medicines on time Don’t do heavy exercise for 3–4 weeks
Sleep with head elevated Don’t wear glasses on the nasal bridge
Attend follow-up visits regularly Don’t expose your nose to direct sunlight

Risks of Rhinoplasty

Like any surgery, Rhinoplasty has some possible risks such as swelling, temporary breathing difficulty, minor bleeding, infection, or need for revision surgery. At ORL International Hospital, strict safety protocols minimize these risks.

Benefits of Rhinoplasty

  • Better nose shape and symmetry

  • Improved facial balance

  • Enhanced breathing function

  • Correction of birth defects or injuries

  • Better confidence and appearance

  • More defined nasal tip and bridge

Advantages at ORL International Hospital

  • ENT experts with years of Rhinoplasty experience

  • Modern technology and advanced surgical methods

  • Focus on natural-looking results

  • Personalized treatment planning

  • Safe environment with complete care and follow-up support

Rhinoplasty Surgery, commonly known as a nose job, is a procedure that reshapes or corrects the nose to improve its appearance and breathing function. It can refine the nose tip, smooth a nasal hump, straighten the nose, or enhance the overall facial balance. Rhinoplasty also helps correct structural problems that cause breathing issues. The goal is to create a natural-looking nose that suits your face while improving comfort and confidence.
